Select tenders, then run an eligibility check.

01 · Analysis

Every document in a selected tender is downloaded and read, including files inside ZIP archives. Each eligibility criterion is compared with the company profile. A tender usually takes a few minutes.

The criteria found are saved with the documents. Checking the tender again compares the profile with them without reading the documents; switch on Regenerate criteria to read them again.

02 · Runs in background

You can close this page while checks run on the server. Notify me when done sends a browser notification (localhost and https only). Verdicts and shortlist and drop decisions are kept when the server restarts.

03 · Verdicts

  • Eligible — the company meets every criterion.
  • Not eligible — a criterion is not met; the row names it.
  • Needs review — a criterion couldn't be determined, a file was unreadable, or no criteria were found. Open it before deciding.

The verdict is for the company bidding alone. The line under it gives the outcome as lead member of a joint venture (JV), when one may bid. The analysis compares the two criterion by criterion; a green row is one the company misses alone but meets as JV lead member.
